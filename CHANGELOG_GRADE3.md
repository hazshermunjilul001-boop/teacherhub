# Grade 3 SF9 and One-Page Update

A dedicated elementary SF9 layout now serves both Grade 2 and Grade 3. Grade 3 follows the supplied one-page reference form with Filipino, English, Mathematics, Science, GMRC / Values Education, and Makabansa, plus the merged TERM grade-table heading and the matching attendance, comments, signatures, transfer, and descriptor sections.

The existing Grade 4–10 layout remains unchanged in its grade-band routing. Printable SF9 cards are constrained to one A4 landscape page, and editable DOCX samples for Grades 2 through 10 were rendered and verified at one PDF page each.
